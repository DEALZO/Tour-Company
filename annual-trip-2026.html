Itinerary

Day 01: Depart from Karachi to Islamabad by bus/train.
Day 02: Arrive Islamabad, hotel check-in.
Day 03: Islamabad to Naran. Rafting at River Kunhar.
Day 04: Naran to Skardu via Babusar Top.
Day 05: Khaplu Valley, Chaqchan Mosque, Crystal Lake.
Day 06: Hussainabad Waterfall, Kharpocho Fort, Organic Village.
Day 07: Basho Valley 4x4, Camping & Bonfire.
Day 08: SOQ Valley, Upper Kachura Lake, Shangrila Resort.
Day 09: Skardu to Naran.
Day 10: Naran to Islamabad. Khanpur Lake.
Day 11: Depart Islamabad for Karachi.
Day 12: Arrive Karachi.
