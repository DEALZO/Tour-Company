ANNUAL TRIP 3.0

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey to the breathtaking landscapes of Naran, Skardu, Khaplu Valley, Basho Valley, and Kachura. This exciting tour combines scenic mountain views, peaceful lakes, historic landmarks, lush valleys, and adventurous experiences. From rafting at River Kunhar and exploring Naran to discovering the beauty of Khaplu, visiting Chaqchan Mosque and Crystal Lake Sogha, and exploring Kharpocho Fort and Organic Village, every day offers something new to experience.

The adventure continues with an exciting camping and bonfire experience in the stunning Basho Valley, followed by exploration of SOQ Valley, Upper Kachura Lake, and Shangrila Resort. Enjoy the natural beauty of the northern areas while traveling through some of Pakistan's most spectacular mountain landscapes.

Itinerary +
Day 01 – Friday: Depart from Karachi and travel to Islamabad by bus/train. Flight passengers will depart the following day.
Day 02 – Saturday: Arrive in Islamabad/Rawalpindi by evening and transfer to the hotel for check-in and rest. Enjoy dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.
Day 03 – Sunday: Depart early morning for Naran with breakfast at Balakot and short stops at Kewai Waterfall and Kaghan. Enjoy Naran Bazaar and rafting at River Kunhar before dinner and overnight stay.
Day 04 – Monday: Travel from Naran to Skardu via Babusar Top, Lulusar Lake, Batakundi and Three Mountain Junction. Arrive in Skardu by night for dinner and overnight stay.
Day 05 – Tuesday: Explore the scenic Khaplu Valley, including Ghawari Farms, Chaqchan Mosque and Crystal Lake Sogha. Return to Skardu in the evening for dinner and overnight stay.
Day 06 – Wednesday: Visit Hussainabad Waterfall before exploring the historic Kharpocho Fort. Trek from the fort towards Organic Village and return to Skardu in the evening.
Day 07 – Thursday: Travel to the stunning Basho Valley by 4×4 Prado with a stop at the Chocolate Mountains. Enjoy the meadows and forest followed by bonfire, dinner and overnight camping.
Day 08 – Friday: Explore SOQ Valley and Upper Kachura Lake with leisure time and optional boating. Visit Shangrila Resort at Lower Kachura Lake before returning to the hotel.
Day 09 – Saturday: Depart Skardu for Naran via Babusar Top with scenic short stops along the way. Arrive in Naran by late evening for dinner and overnight stay.
Day 10 – Sunday: Travel from Naran to Islamabad with a short stop at Balakot and a visit to Khanpur Lake. Enjoy optional water sports before reaching Islamabad in the evening.
Day 11 – Monday: Check out from the hotel and travel to the bus terminal or railway station. Depart Islamabad/Rawalpindi for Karachi by bus, train or optional flight.
Day 12 – Tuesday: Arrive back in Karachi after completing the tour. End of the journey with unforgettable memories of Naran, Skardu, Khaplu and Basho Valley.
